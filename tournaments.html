Tournament Rules & Format

Fair play, transparent brackets, and professional standards

📋

General Rules

  • All participants must register before deadline
  • Valid ID required for prize collection
  • No cheating, exploits, or unauthorized software
  • Admins' decisions are final
  • Disputes resolved within 15 minutes
🏆

Prize Distribution

  • Prizes awarded within 24 hours
  • Cash prizes via UPI/Bank Transfer
  • Physical prizes collected at venue
  • Team prizes split equally among members
  • Trophies/medals for 1st-3rd place
⚙️

Technical Standards

  • All matches on LAN (zero ping)
  • Standardized hardware per station
  • Peripherals provided (bring your own mouse allowed)
  • Warm-up time: 10 mins before match
  • Pause allowed only for technical issues
📅

Schedule & Format

  • Check-in: 30 mins before start
  • Late arrival = forfeit (5 min grace)
  • Best of 1 for groups, BO3 for playoffs
  • Grand Finals: BO5 (Valorant/CS2)
  • Streamed on our YouTube/Discord
